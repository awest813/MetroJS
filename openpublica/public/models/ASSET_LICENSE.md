# Building models

GLB kits the renderer uses in place of the procedural kits on High quality.
A building def names its model with `visualRef` in `src/data/buildings.json`;
a def without one, a model that fails to load, and Low quality all use the
procedural kit (`src/render/buildingVisuals.ts`).

## Adding or replacing a model

Any glTF 2.0 binary works. Export it to these conventions:

- One unit is one map tile. The model stands on y = 0, centred on its
  origin, inside the tile (a building usually leaves a margin: the small
  house is 0.5 × 0.5). The loader re-centres a model authored off its origin
  and shrinks one wider than 0.96 or taller than 2.
- Its front (door, shopfront) faces +Z; the renderer turns it to its street.
- Colours come from each material's base colour, times its base-colour
  texture sampled at each vertex, times any vertex colours. A palette-texture
  kit (one small swatch image) comes through exactly; a detailed texture is
  reduced to its colour at each vertex. Normals are required.
- Keep it light: every building of a kind draws its model, so aim for a few
  hundred triangles or fewer.

Then add a row to the table above with the file, where it came from (author
and URL), and its licence. Use only models you may redistribute under a
licence compatible with GPL-3.0 (CC0, CC-BY with the attribution recorded
here, or your own work). Do not add art from SimCity or any other Electronic
Arts product.
